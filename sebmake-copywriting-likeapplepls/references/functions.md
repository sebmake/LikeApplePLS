# Phrases by function

Templates, never quotes: no sentence here is taken from a keynote. Each is the **shape** the corpus uses for one job, with its slots in brackets. Pick the function, fill the slots with the user's words, then vary from one block to the next (the corpus repeats a shape, not a sentence). Counts in `grammar.md`; the French forms and register in `french.md`.

Register marks: a single form holds for every register. **P-tu** = the reader addressed as *tu*; **casual** = the brand that says *on*.

## 1. Opening and framing

| EN | FR | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Today, [X] [does A]. But [obstacle]. | Aujourd'hui, [X] [fait A]. Mais [obstacle]. | tension in two sentences; the second is short |
| At [company], [value] [has always been / is] [X]. | Chez [entreprise], [valeur], c'est [X]. | thesis; one per text |
| Our goal was simple: [verb] [X]. | Notre but était simple : [verbe] [X]. | frames a block |
| This year, we're [verb]-ing [X]. | Cette année, nous [verbe] [X]. · casual: Cette année, on [verbe] [X]. | time marker |
| We have [a lot] to [show you]. | Nous avons [beaucoup] à vous [montrer]. · P-tu: On a [plein de choses] à te [montrer]. | announces the menu; spoken or email, not on a page |
| What if you could [X]? | Et si vous pouviez [X] ? · P-tu: Et si tu pouvais [X] ? | the one rhetorical question a text may have |

In an **email**, the opening line is the news itself (§ 2 or § 4), never a greeting formula. On a **page**, the hero is § 2.

## 2. Naming and verdict

| EN | FR | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Introducing [Name]. | Voici [Nom]. | bare form; the name alone, then the verdict |
| Meet [Name]. | Découvrez [Nom]. | on screen or hero; not in a formal email |
| Today we're [introducing / launching] [X]. | Aujourd'hui, nous lançons [X]. · casual: Aujourd'hui, on lance [X]. | full spoken form |
| And we call it… [Name]. | Et nous l'avons appelé… [Nom]. · casual: On l'a appelé… [Nom]. | suspended name; the only ellipsis allowed |
| We're [proud] to [introduce] [X]. | Nous avons [le plaisir] de [vous présenter] [X]. · casual: On est [fiers] de [vous présenter] [X]. | FR: pleasure, not excitement; never « ravis » (0 in the FR corpus) |
| [Name] is the [simple] way to [benefit] [without pain]. | [Nom] est le moyen [simple] de [bénéfice] [sans douleur]. | **the verdict sentence**: definition in 8 to 14 words |
| [Name] is [truly] [adj]. | [Nom] est [vraiment] [adj]. | the host's verdict; no “I think” |
| [Name]. [Benefit], [benefit], [benefit]. | [Nom]. [Bénéfice], [bénéfice], [bénéfice]. | hero in two units: name, then a triad of nouns |
| The [all-new] [X]. | Le [tout nouveau] [X]. | nominal headline for a page or slide |

## 3. Tension (the problem)

| EN | FR | Note |
|---|---|---|
| [Today], [doing X] means [cost]. | [Aujourd'hui], [faire X], c'est [coût]. | one concrete cost, no adjective |
| You [verb] [X]. Then you [verb] [Y]. Then… | Vous [verbe] [X]. Puis vous [verbe] [Y]. Puis… | the chore, step by step; three steps at most |
| And if [condition], [consequence]. | Et si [condition], [conséquence]. | the risk, in one line |
| [X] shouldn't be [hard / this hard]. | [X] ne devrait pas être [si compliqué]. | the pivot to the solution |

Rules: the tension is stated, not dramatized (no “nightmare”, no “frustrating”); it is the reader's situation, in the second person; it lasts two to four sentences, then the name arrives.

## 4. Feature, then the turn to the benefit

The corpus never leaves a feature as a feature: a turn follows, about once per block.

| EN | FR | Note |
|---|---|---|
| [Name] [verb]s [object]. So you can [benefit]. | [Nom] [verbe] [objet]. Vous pouvez donc [bénéfice]. / Pour [bénéfice]. | **the standard pair**: fact, then gain |
| [Feature], which means [benefit]. | [Fonction], ce qui veut dire [bénéfice]. | in one sentence |
| Now you can [verb] [object] [right from X]. | Vous pouvez désormais [verbe] [objet] [directement depuis X]. · P-tu: Tu peux désormais… | the new ability |
| Whether you're [situation A] or [situation B], [X] [verb]s. | Que vous soyez [situation A] ou [situation B], [X] [verbe]. | two readers, one sentence |
| No more [chore]. | Fini [la corvée]. | the negative benefit; short, after a fact |
| [X] does the [work] for you. | [X] fait [le travail] à votre place. | |
| All in [one place / X]. · All with [X]. | Tout ça, dans [X]. · Le tout, avec [X]. | the wrap after a list |
| Not only [A], but [B]. | Non seulement [A], mais [B]. | escalation; once per text |
| It's that [simple / fast]. | C'est aussi [simple / rapide] que ça. | closes a how-it-works |

A feature headline (page block, slide) is **a verb phrase of 2 to 5 words**: *[Verb] [object] [in one tap].* The proof sentence follows; the turn closes.

## 5. Figures and comparisons

| EN | FR | Note |
|---|---|---|
| [N] times [faster] than [base]. | [N] fois plus [rapide] que [base]. | the base in the same sentence, always |
| Up to [N] [unit], [condition]. | Jusqu'à [N] [unité], [condition]. | the condition in the same sentence or the label |
| [N] more [unit] than [base]. | [N] [unité] de plus que [base]. | base: the previous version, preferably |
| [N] % [faster] than [base]. | [N] % plus [adj] que [base]. | FR: non-breaking space before % |
| That's enough to [concrete equivalent]. | De quoi [équivalent concret]. | the figure turned into life; one per figure at most |
| Over [N] [million] [items] every [period]. | Plus de [N] [millions] de [objets] chaque [période]. | usage figure |
| From [old value] to [new value]. | De [ancienne valeur] à [nouvelle valeur]. | the before / after in four words |

Rules: one or two figures per announcement; each one with its base; the source on the final card or under the figure, as the audience requires (`publics.md` of the keynote skill); never a figure the user did not provide (`[TO PROVIDE]`).

## 6. Superlatives, by level

| Level | EN | FR | Condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — relative to yourself | our most [adj] [X] yet | notre [X] le plus [adj] à ce jour | true against your own versions |
| 1 | the [adj-est] [X] we've ever [made] | le [X] le plus [adj] que nous ayons jamais [conçu] | same; for a product |
| 1 | [adj-er] than ever | plus [adj] que jamais | same |
| 2 — a first | for the first time, [X] | pour la première fois, [X] | must be true; add “for us” / « chez nous » if in doubt |
| 3 — absolute | the world's [first / fastest] [X] | le [premier / plus rapide] [X] au monde | external proof, cited next to it |
| 3 | [industry-leading / best-in-class] [X] | la meilleure [X] du marché | external proof, cited |

One superlative per announcement, at most; **the corpus runs about one sentence in twelve with a superlative adjective, and the written media take less** (one per page, one per email). A superlative on a plain description (*our beautiful dashboard*) is cut: the adjective goes on the claim that earns it.

## 7. How it works (the written demo)

The demo, in writing, is the sequence of what the reader does, in the present, in the second person.

| EN | FR | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Just [gesture], and [result]. | Il suffit de [geste], et [résultat]. | the one-step how; “just” is the corpus's favorite word for it |
| [Verb] [object]. [Verb] [object]. Done. | [Verbe] [objet]. [Verbe] [objet]. C'est fait. | two or three imperatives, then the close |
| Here's how it works: [step], [step], [step]. | Voici comment ça marche : [étape], [étape], [étape]. | the triad of steps |
| As soon as [event], [X] [verb]s. | Dès que [événement], [X] [verbe]. | the automatic part |
| You'll see [what appears]. | Vous voyez [ce qui apparaît]. | present in FR even where EN uses “you'll” |
| Take a look. | Regardez. | before a screenshot or a video |

## 8. Transitions and bonus

| EN | FR | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Now, let's talk about [X]. | Parlons maintenant de [X]. | universal connector, spoken; in print, a heading does the job |
| Next, [X]. | Ensuite, [X]. | block change |
| And that's not all: [bonus]. | Et ce n'est pas tout : [bonus]. | opens the best argument, once |
| [Before we close], one last [thing]: [X]. | [Avant de terminer], une dernière [chose] : [X]. | once; never the signature phrase of the brand that made it famous, nor its translation |
| And [X]. | Et [X]. | the thread opener: one sentence in eight starts with “And” in the corpus; in print, keep one or two per text |

## 9. Proof from others

| EN | FR | Note |
|---|---|---|
| We've been working with [partner] to [X]. | Nous travaillons avec [partenaire] pour [X]. | real partner, agreement obtained |
| Here's what [client / outlet] says about [X]. | Voici ce qu'en dit [client / média]. | real, authorized text follows |
| [N] [teams] already [verb] [X]. | [N] [équipes] [verbe] déjà [X]. | the usage figure as proof; with source |

The skill never writes a testimonial, not even as a proposal: `[TO PROVIDE: client testimonial — who, about what, agreement obtained]`.

## 10. Price, availability, ask

| EN | FR | Note |
|---|---|---|
| [Product] starts at [price]. | [Produit] est disponible à partir de [prix]. | anchor only if true; tax status and period stated |
| [X] is available [today / date]. | [X] est disponible [dès aujourd'hui / le date]. | |
| [X] is coming [later this year]. | [X] arrive [plus tard dans l'année]. | |
| [X] is included [with Y] at no extra cost. | [X] est inclus [dans Y], sans surcoût. | |
| Pricing and details on [date]. | Tarifs et détails le [date]. | when the price is not set |
| **Ask:** [Verb] [object]. | [Verbe] [objet]. | 1 to 4 words, verb first: *Order today* · *Try it free* · *See the plans* · *Reply to this email* |

## 11. Recap

| Level | EN | FR |
|---|---|---|
| a feature | So that's [X]. | Voilà pour [X]. |
| a product or block | So that's [Name]: [A], [B] and [C]. | Voilà pour [Nom] : [A], [B] et [C]. |
| a product | And that's [Name], [one-line verdict]. | Voilà [Nom], [définition d'une ligne]. |
| a lineup | Our [all-new] [X] lineup. | Notre [toute nouvelle] gamme [X]. |
| the whole | What a [big] [day / release] for [X]. | Quelle [journée / version] pour [X] ! |

In print, the recap is the last line of a page section or the P.S. of an email: the triad of nouns, nothing new.

## 13. The turns (short devices used everywhere)

| Device | EN | FR | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| the landing fragment | *[Noun].* · *Done.* · *That's it.* | *[Nom].* · *C'est fait.* · *Et voilà.* | a sentence of 1 to 4 words after a long one; about one sentence in twenty in the corpus |
| the triad | [A], [B] and [C] | [A], [B] et [C] | one sentence in seven in English, one in ten in French; nouns of the same kind, same length |
| the pair | [A] and [B]. | [A] et [B]. | French prefers it; English stacks three |
| the echo | repeat the exact word of the thesis in the recap | same | never a synonym for the key term |
| the contrast | [Not X]. [Y]. | [Pas X]. [Y]. | two fragments, the second is the point |
| the right-here | right from [X] · right on [device] | directement depuis [X] | the place where it happens |
| the same-sentence base | [N]× … than [base] | [N] fois … que [base] | never a comparison without its base |
| the bounded best | our … yet · … than ever | … à ce jour · … que jamais | never “the best” without proof |
| the ask verb | [Verb] [object] | [Verbe] [objet] | never “click here”, “submit”, “OK” |
